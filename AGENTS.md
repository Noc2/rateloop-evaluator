# Contributor boundaries

- All original software in this repository is Apache-2.0. Do not copy proprietary RateLoop application code.
- Keep raw inputs, human feedback, credentials, weights and private training outputs out of Git.
- No model downloads or external telemetry during inference or training. Provision explicitly, then use pinned local paths.
- AI use, private training and shared contribution are independent rights. Fail closed on missing or expired grants.
- Preserve independent human labels. AI outputs and exposed-to-AI approvals are not independent training references.
- Test contract validation, workspace isolation, revocation, retries and checkpoint round trips.
- The primary agent coordinates commits. Do not stage, commit, publish or modify files owned by another agent.
